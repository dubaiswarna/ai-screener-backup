# 💎 TREASURE SIGNAL CONFIDENCE GUIDE

## 🎯 WHY NO SIGNALS IN BULL MARKET?

In a strong bull market, most stocks are:
- **Overbought** (RSI > 70)
- **Above all supports** (no buy setups near support)
- **Below resistances** (sell setups have poor R:R)
- **Trending up** (no reversals)

**Result**: 85% confidence filter is TOO STRICT for current market conditions!

## 📈 WHY DIFFERENT MARKETS NEED DIFFERENT SETTINGS?

### Bull Market (Current)
- Stocks are **expensive** → Need to lower confidence
- Few **buy opportunities** → Most signals are momentum-based
- High **RSI** everywhere → Technical layer scores low
- **Solution**: 75-80% confidence captures bullish momentum

### Bear Market
- Stocks are **cheap** → Many buy setups
- Oversold **RSI** → Technical layer scores high
- Near **strong supports** → S&R layer scores high
- **Result**: 85%+ confidence is EASY to achieve

### Sideways Market
- Clear **S&R levels** → Best for treasure signals
- Multiple **reversals** → All 3 layers align
- **Perfect** for 85%+ confidence

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Don't chase quantity**: 5 good signals > 50 average signals
2. **Adjust with market**: Bull = lower confidence, Bear = higher confidence
3. **Use pattern filter**: In bull market, add pattern filter for extra confirmation
4. **Check R:R ratio**: In bull market, lower R:R to 1.5 (targets are harder to reach)
5. **Be patient**: Even at 75%, you might get 0 signals if ALL stocks are extended
