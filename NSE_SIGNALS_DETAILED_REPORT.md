## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: THESE ARE SHORT SELL SIGNALS!

### **What is Short Selling?**

**Regular Trading (BUY):**
- Buy at ₹100 → Sell at ₹103 → Profit ₹3

**Short Selling (SELL):**
- Borrow & Sell at ₹100 → Buy back at ₹97 → Profit ₹3
- **You profit when price FALLS!**

### **How to Short Sell in India:**

**Option 1: Intraday (MIS)**
- Short sell in morning
- Buy back before 3:30 PM
- No actual borrowing needed
- Lower margin required

**Option 2: Derivatives (F&O)**
- Sell futures contract
- Can hold multiple days
- Requires futures account

**Option 3: Stock Lending (SLB)**
- Borrow actual shares
- Sell in cash market
- Advanced traders only
