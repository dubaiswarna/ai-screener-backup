## 🎯 **CURRENT METRICS EXPLAINED:**

### **1. Avg Return/Trade: 3.08%**
**What it is:** Average return PER INDIVIDUAL TRADE

**Calculation:**
```
Trade 1: +5.2%
Trade 2: -3.1%
Trade 3: +8.5%
Trade 4: +1.2%
...
Trade 323: +4.1%

Average = Sum of all returns / 323 = 3.08%
```

**What it tells you:** On average, each trade makes 3.08%

---

### **2. Total Portfolio Return: 49.30%** ⭐ (The Important One!)
**What it is:** TOTAL return on your ENTIRE PORTFOLIO

**Calculation:**
```
Initial Capital: Rs 40,00,000 (Investment × Max Portfolio)
Total P&L: Rs 19,72,012
Total Return: Rs 19,72,012 / Rs 40,00,000 = 49.30%
```

**What it tells you:** Your portfolio grew by 49.30% over the entire period

---

### **3. Annualized Return: 16.43%** ⭐ NEW! (What You Asked For!)
**What it is:** Average return PER YEAR

**Calculation:**
```
Total Return: 49.30%
Period: 3 years
Annualized: 49.30% / 3 = 16.43% per year
```

**What it tells you:** Your portfolio grows ~16.43% every year on average

---

### **4. CAGR: 14.32%** ⭐ NEW! (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
**What it is:** True compounded yearly growth rate

**Calculation:**
```
Initial: Rs 40,00,000
Final: Rs 59,72,012
Years: 3
CAGR = ((59,72,012 / 40,00,000)^(1/3) - 1) × 100 = 14.32%
```

**What it tells you:** If you compound returns, portfolio grows 14.32% per year
